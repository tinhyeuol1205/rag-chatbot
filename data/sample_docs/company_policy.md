# Company Policies — TechCorp Inc.

## 1. Remote Work Policy

### 1.1 Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probation period (3 months) are eligible for remote work. Contract workers and interns must receive explicit approval from their department head before working remotely.

### 1.2 Work Schedule
Remote employees must maintain standard working hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (local time zone), with a mandatory 1-hour lunch break. Flexible schedules may be arranged with manager approval, provided at least 4 core hours (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM) overlap with team members.

### 1.3 Equipment and Setup
The company will provide a laptop (Dell XPS or MacBook Pro, based on department allocation), an external monitor, and a headset for remote work. Employees are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection with a minimum speed of 50 Mbps download. IT support can be reached at help@techcorp.com or ext. 5555.

### 1.4 Communication Requirements
Remote workers must be available on Slack during working hours and respond to messages within 30 minutes. All cameras must be turned on during video meetings. Daily standups are mandatory and should not exceed 15 minutes.

## 2. Leave and Time-Off Policy

### 2.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued at 1.25 days per month. Unused leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days to the next calendar year. Any leave beyond the carry-over limit will be forfeited on December 31st.

### 2.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over to the next year and is not compensated upon termination.

### 2.3 Parental Leave
New parents (both maternity and paternity) are eligible for 6 months of fully paid parental leave. This applies to both biological and adoptive parents. Employees must notify HR at least 60 days before the expected leave start date.

### 2.4 Public Holidays
TechCorp observes 12 public holidays annually, as published in the company calendar at the beginning of each year. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday will be designated as the substitute holiday.

## 3. Onboarding Process

### 3.1 Pre-Arrival
Before the first day, HR will send a welcome email containing: (1) employment contract for digital signature, (2) IT account setup instructions, (3) first-week schedule, and (4) a list of required documents (national ID, bank account details, tax forms).

### 3.2 First Day
New employees will receive their laptop and security badge at the reception desk by 9:00 AM. An IT orientation session will cover: email setup, VPN access, Slack workspace, Jira project boards, and the internal knowledge base (Confluence). Lunch will be provided with the onboarding cohort.

### 3.3 First Week
The assigned mentor will guide the new employee through: (1) team introduction and role expectations, (2) codebase walkthrough for engineering roles, (3) access to relevant Notion documents and Figma files, and (4) setting up the local development environment (Docker, VS Code, Git).

### 3.4 Probation Period
The probation period lasts 3 months. During this time, the employee will have monthly check-ins with their manager and HR. At the end of probation, a formal review determines continuation of employment. Probation may be extended by 1 month in exceptional cases.

## 4. Information Security Policy

### 4.1 Password Requirements
All passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and cannot repeat the last 5 passwords. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

### 4.2 Data Classification
Company data is classified into four levels: (1) Public — freely shareable, (2) Internal — for employees only, (3) Confidential — restricted to specific teams, and (4) Restricted — requires explicit access approval from the CISO. All documents must be labeled with their classification level.

### 4.3 Device Security
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption (BitLocker or FileVault) enabled at all times. Auto-lock must be set to activate after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB storage devices are disabled by default; exceptions require CISO approval.

### 4.4 Incident Reporting
Any suspected security breach must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery. Reports can be submitted via email to soc@techcorp.com or by calling the 24/7 hotline at +1-800-SEC-HELP. Failure to report incidents may result in disciplinary action.

## 5. Expense and Reimbursement Policy

### 5.1 Travel Expenses
Business travel must be pre-approved by the department manager. Economy class flights are standard for domestic travel; business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 8 hours. Hotel accommodations should not exceed $200/night for domestic travel and $300/night for international travel.

### 5.2 Meal Allowances
Daily meal allowances during business travel: $50 for domestic trips and $80 for international trips. Team dinners require prior manager approval and are capped at $75 per person. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

### 5.3 Reimbursement Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the SAP Concur platform within 30 days of the expense date. Original receipts (or clear photos) must be attached for all expenses exceeding $25. Reimbursements are processed within 14 business days after approval.

### 5.4 Professional Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000 for conferences, courses, and certifications. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Requests for amounts exceeding the budget require VP-level approval.
